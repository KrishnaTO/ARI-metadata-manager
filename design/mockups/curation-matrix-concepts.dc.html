1 Simplifying cross-reference curation in the matrix

Real ARI data throughout (diseases and ids from ari.sssom.tsv and ari.predicted.sssom.tsv). Two directions, each in light and dark: 1a keeps the familiar spreadsheet grid with ids on show; 1c trades ids for state so all ten databases fit, and opens one disease at a time. No bulk verdicts anywhere — every judgment is made by a curator on a single mapping.
