Autoimmune retinal vasculitis is an inflammatory condition affecting the blood vessels of the retina that arises without an associated systemic rheumatic disease. Inflammation and occlusion of retinal vessels can cause blurred vision, blind spots, floaters, and potential retinal ischemia. Treatment involves localized and systemic corticosteroids and immunomodulatory agents to preserve eyesight.
